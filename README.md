# 💥 Cartoon RPG: Multiverse Mayhem

Welcome to **Cartoon RPG: Multiverse Mayhem**, a web-based, turn-based RPG featuring iconic cartoon heroes and villains. Fight through classic show locations using cartoon logic, special abilities, and dice rolls!

---

## 🕹️ Game Rules & Mechanics

### 1. Stats & Checks
Actions use a standard **d20 dice system**. When you perform an action, your roll is added to your character's stat modifier:
$$\text{Total Result} = \text{d20 Roll} + \text{Stat Modifier}$$

* **DC (Difficulty Class) 10:** Easy Action
* **DC 15:** Moderate Challenge
* **DC 20:** Legendary Move

### 2. Core Stats
* **Smarts:** Increases technical gadgets, improvised crafting, and tactical analysis (Pickle Rick, Stewie, Batman).
* **Agility:** Determines turn order, dodge chance, and speed-based tricks (Road Runner, Quagmire).
* **Strength:** Controls physical attack damage and heavy checks (Taz, Batman).
* **Goofiness:** Affects unpredictable abilities, comedy dodges, and wild status effects.

### 3. Combat & Rest
* **Combat:** Turn-based battles where team members use standard attacks or signature abilities.
* **Resting:** Returning to the **Simpson House Couch** completely restores all party HP.

## 🗺️ World Locations

* **🏡 Simpson House:** The safe home base and starting hub.
* **🌵 Desert Highway:** Road Runner's domain; watch out for falling ACME anvils!
* **🌳 Whispering Woods:** A mysterious forest packed with rogue cartoon critters.
* **🛸 Galactic Federation Ship:** High-tech orbital base full of security lasers and alien loot.
* **🔥 Robot Hell:** Underworld dungeon requiring high Goofiness to escape!
